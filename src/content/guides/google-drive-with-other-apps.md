Google Drive works with Microsoft Office files, other Google apps, Marketplace tools, Maps, and (on Android) device backups. This guide covers the pieces that show up most often on school Chromebooks and related devices.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

For syncing a Windows or Mac computer with Drive, see [Use Google Drive for desktop](/guide/use-google-drive-for-desktop).

## Microsoft Office files on the web

You can upload and work on Office files with Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides in a browser.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

On the web you can:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- Open, edit, and save Office files in Drive
- Convert Office files to Google Docs, Sheets, or Slides
- Comment on Office files in Drive preview
- Edit Office files with the **Office Compatibility Mode** Chrome extension

When you upload an Office file, you can edit, comment, and collaborate in Docs, Sheets, or Slides. Changes auto-save in the original Office format.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Convert to a Google file if you need features such as Add-ons, Apps Script, protected ranges, or translate. Converting makes a **copy** of the Office file.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Chrome extension tip:** If you open a file in Office editing mode, disable the Office Compatibility Mode extension to avoid compatibility issues.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Office files with Drive for desktop

If Drive for desktop is installed on a Windows or Mac computer, you can keep working in Word, Excel, or PowerPoint while files sync to Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

1. Install Google Drive for desktop.
2. Add your Office file to your Google Drive folder.
3. Double-click the file.
4. Edit it. Changes sync to Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

With Microsoft Office 2010 or later, **real-time presence** can alert you when others edit the same shared Office file. Status options include Safe to Edit, Wait to Edit, and New Version Created.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

On Windows with a work or school account, Drive for desktop can also send and save files through Microsoft Outlook (Outlook 2010 or later, Windows only).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Add Drive photos to Google Photos

You can add photos from Drive to Google Photos to organize them across services.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

Before you start:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- The photo must be larger than **256 pixels**
- Supported types include `.jpg`, `.heic`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.gif`, and most RAW files
- If you use a Google Account through **work or school**, download from Drive and re-upload to Photos

Changes in Drive apply only to Drive. Changes in Photos apply only to Photos.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Use Google products side by side

On a computer, you can open other Google tools in the same window as Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Chat, Docs, Sheets, or Slides without jumping between tabs.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

1. Open Drive (or Gmail, Calendar, Chat, Docs, Sheets, or Slides).
2. At the bottom right, click **Show side panel**.
3. Choose a product on the right, such as:
   - **Calendar** — schedule and events
   - **Keep** — notes or lists
   - **Tasks** — to-dos and deadlines
   - **Contacts**
   - **Voice**
   - **Maps**
4. To close the panel, click **Close**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Google Drive apps from the Marketplace

Download apps from the Google Workspace Marketplace to edit images and video, fax and sign documents, create flow charts, and more in Drive. After you add an app, you can use it with Drive on the web in supported browsers.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Create a file with an app

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. On the left, click **New**.
3. Choose an app.
4. After you save, find the file in **My Drive**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Not all Drive apps can create files in Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Open a file with an app

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Right-click a file.
3. Point to **Open with**.
4. Select an app.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

To set a default app for certain file types: **Settings** → **Settings** → **Manage apps** → check **Use by default** next to the app.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Files from Drive apps save when you edit them or choose options such as **Export to Google Drive**, **Save a copy**, or **Send to Drive**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Many Marketplace apps are made by other companies with their own terms and privacy policies. To disconnect an app: **Settings** → **Manage apps** → **Options** → **Disconnect from Drive**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Android device backups

On Android, you can back up content, data, and settings to your Google Account and restore them to the same phone or some other Android phones.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

You **cannot** use this backup when you set up a personal device with a work profile or for work only, or when you set up a company-owned device.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Restoring varies by phone and Android version. You cannot restore a backup from a higher Android version onto a phone running a lower version. Some steps work only on Android 9 and up.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

To turn automatic backup on or off:

1. Open the device **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Google services** → **All services**.
3. Turn **Backup** on or off.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

A Google One backup can take up to 24 hours. Google publishes a free backup allowance of up to **15 GB** for your Google Account, with more storage available through Google One.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

## Google My Maps in Drive

You can see My Maps you created and maps shared with you in Drive, and manage them with Drive features.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

### Create or open a map (computer)

1. Sign in to your Google Account and open Drive.
2. Click **New** → **More** → **Google My Maps**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

To open an existing map: **My Drive** → double-click the map.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

### Share, rename, or remove

- **Share:** Select the map → **Share** → add people and choose **Can view** or **Can edit** (for maps, **Can comment** works like **Can view**). Sharing changes in Drive also apply in My Maps.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>
- **Rename / description:** Select the map → **More actions** → **Rename** or **View details**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>
- **Remove:** Select the map → **Remove**. It goes to Trash; it still appears in My Maps until you permanently delete it from Trash.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Maps do **not** use your Drive storage quota. They are available only in Drive on the web — not offline.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

You cannot create My Maps on mobile. On a phone or tablet you can open a map in the Drive app and view it in the browser, or remove it from the file menu.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>
