Your Google Account includes up to **15 GB** of storage shared across **Google Drive**, **Gmail**, and **Google Photos**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup> When that space fills up, uploads, email, and photo backups can stop working.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## What counts toward your storage

These items use your Google Account storage:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- Files in Google Drive (PDFs, images, videos, and more)
- Gmail messages and attachments, including Spam and Trash
- Original quality photos and videos backed up to Google Photos
- Storage saver / Express quality Photos backups added after June 1, 2021
- Meet recordings
- Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drawings, Forms, Recorder, and Vids files created or edited after June 1, 2021
- WhatsApp backups on Android
- Android device backups (messages, call history, settings, and app data)

What usually does **not** use your storage:

- Files in **Shared with me** (they count against the owner’s storage, not yours)<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- Files in a shared folder that someone else owns<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

If you make a **copy** of a shared file, or add your own files into a shared folder, those new files use **your** storage.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Items in Drive Trash still count toward storage until they are permanently deleted. See [Delete files in Google Drive](/guide/delete-files-google-drive).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## When you run out of space

If you go over your storage quota:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- You cannot upload new files to Google Drive
- You cannot back up new photos or videos to Google Photos
- Sending and receiving email in Gmail can stop working
- You cannot create new Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drawings, Forms, or Vids files — and others cannot edit or copy your affected files until you free space
- You cannot back up new Recorder files

You can still sign in and open existing content.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

If you stay over quota for **2 years**, Google may remove content across Gmail, Drive, Photos, and Android backup. Google says it gives at least **3 months** of notice first, and you can free space, buy more storage, or download your data before that happens.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

After you delete a lot of files, storage numbers can take **48–72 hours** to update.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Free up space (short-term)

Emptying Trash and Spam is often the fastest way to get critical services (like Gmail) working again. Until you permanently clear those folders, their contents still count against your limit.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

1. Empty **Gmail** Trash and Spam.
2. Empty **Drive** Trash (see [Delete files in Google Drive](/guide/delete-files-google-drive)).
3. Empty **Google Photos** Trash.
4. In Gmail, delete a few messages with very large attachments if you no longer need them.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

After you delete and empty Trash, wait a bit for available space to refresh. If the account is still full after about **30 minutes**, try the cleanup tips below.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

Need a file you removed? Check [Recover a deleted file in Google Drive](/guide/recover-deleted-file-google-drive) or [Find files in Google Drive](/guide/find-files-google-drive).

## Clear space (longer-term)

### Find large files in Drive

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. On the left, click **Storage**.
3. Files list from largest to smallest — remove what you no longer need.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Find large emails in Gmail

In the Gmail search bar, try:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- `has:attachment larger:10M` — messages with attachments over 10 MB
- `filename:.pdf larger:5M` — PDF attachments over 5 MB
- `older_than:2y has:attachment` — older messages with attachments

### Look for duplicates in Drive

Google Drive does not auto-find duplicates. Switch to list view, sort by **Name**, and watch for copies ending in `(1)` or starting with `Copy of`.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Other storage (backups and apps)

Device backups and hidden app data can also use space. Google’s storage manager includes a **Backups** section, and Drive settings has **Manage apps** for connected app data.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### WhatsApp backups (Android)

WhatsApp backups on Android count toward your Google Account storage.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

1. In WhatsApp, open **Settings → Chats → Chat backup** and turn off **Include videos** if you want smaller future backups.
2. Tap **Back Up now** with the new settings.
3. Then remove old backup files in Google One storage management if you no longer need them.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

Deleting a WhatsApp backup is permanent — you can lose chat history if you change phones or reinstall WhatsApp before creating a new backup.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Buy more storage (Google One)

Personal accounts can add storage with a **Google One** membership (plans often start at 100 GB where available).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

**School or work accounts:** you generally cannot buy more storage for yourself. Contact the person who manages your account if you need more space.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

On a personal account (computer):

1. Sign in to your Google Account.
2. Go to [one.google.com](https://one.google.com).
3. On the left, click **Upgrade**.
4. Choose a plan, review the price and payment date, then click **Next**.
5. Click **Subscribe** to confirm.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

After you upgrade, it can take up to **24 hours** for the new storage to appear. Signing out and back in can help. If it has been more than 24 hours, Google One support can help.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel. Buying storage is only available in some countries.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Drive for desktop vs cloud storage

The free space on your computer is separate from your Google Account cloud storage. Drive for desktop may show different numbers than the Drive website — that is expected.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
