Gemini in Google Drive can help you search files in everyday language, summarize content, organize loose files, and create drafts when your account has access.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Important:** To use Gemini in Drive, you need an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. What you see depends on your plan and, for some school or work features, what your administrator turns on.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

Google also publishes notes on [disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790?hl=en), [supported languages](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/14925782?hl=en), and [how Gemini protects your data](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/14615114?hl=en).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-9">9</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

For everyday Drive search without Gemini, see [Find files in Google Drive](/guide/find-files-google-drive). To tidy files yourself, see [Organize files in Google Drive](/guide/organize-files-google-drive).

## Open Ask Gemini

### Computer (including Chromebook)

On the web, Gemini opens in a full-screen workspace.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the top right, click **Ask Gemini**.
3. Use the workspace:
   - **Prompt box** (bottom): type your request.
   - **Menu** (top left): conversation history, Gems, and saved projects.
   - **Sources panel** (left): manage file sources and search settings for other Google Workspace apps.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. At the top right, tap **Ask Gemini**.
3. Select a suggested prompt or type your own question in the prompt box at the bottom.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. At the top right, tap **Ask Gemini**.
3. Select a suggested prompt or type your own question in the prompt box at the bottom.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Search and retrieve files

Instead of only using exact file names, you can ask Gemini with everyday phrases. Google notes this needs an eligible Workspace or Google AI plan.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Natural language in the Drive search bar (computer)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. In the search bar, enter a prompt such as:
   - “Show me the slide decks about the project timeline that Deepti shared with me in Chat.”
   - “Show me the PDF Josh emailed me last week about the marketing strategy.”
3. Click the inline citations to open the source files and check the answer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### AI Overviews in search (computer)

When you ask about the contents of your files, an **AI Overview** may appear at the top of the results with a short summary.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. In the search bar, ask a question such as “What is the Q3 marketing budget?” or “Summarize the ABC project timeline for this year.”
3. Read the overview at the top of the results.
4. Click citations to open the source files.
5. Optional: from the overview, click **Ask Gemini** to continue in the full-screen workspace with that search context.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Tip: You can give feedback on an overview with thumbs up or thumbs down.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Control which apps AI Overviews use (computer)

These settings apply to Gemini AI Overviews and natural-language file finding. They do **not** change standard Drive search results.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. In the search bar, click **AI Overview search**.
3. Check or uncheck sources such as:
   - Drive files
   - Gmail attachments & links
   - Chat uploads & links
   - Calendar attachments & links<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Updates and summaries (Drive web)

Google notes these features need an eligible plan. **Catch me up** is only available on Drive web ([drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com)).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

### Catch me up

If a Google Doc or Sheet shows **Catch me up** next to the file name, there is new information since you last viewed it. Click the badge for an update on changes and comments.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

### Summary cards on PDFs and videos

When you open a PDF or a video with a transcript, a summary card may appear.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

To turn summary cards **off**:

1. Open a PDF or video file.
2. On the summary card or in the file toolbar, click **More**.
3. Click **Disable summary cards**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

To turn them **on** again: open the file → **More** in the toolbar → **Enable summary cards**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

## Let Gemini organize files (Drive web, English)

**Important:** This feature is only available on Drive web and in English.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Gemini can suggest moving loose files into existing folders or new folders. It does not move files until you approve.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Eligible files include PDFs, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Microsoft Office files, images, and videos with transcripts. Gemini can also organize shortcuts. You need **Editor** access to the files.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Open **My Drive** or a folder. If files can be organized, **Suggest file moves** appears.
3. Click **Suggest file moves**.
4. Review suggestions. You can:
   - **Refine** (prompt, organize by project, organize meeting notes, more/fewer new folders, shorten folder names, or try again).
   - **Rename** a suggested folder.
   - **Change location** for a suggested destination.
   - Check files to move → **Move files** → **Create and move**.
   - **Remove** a suggestion (Gemini will not suggest that file–folder pair again for 30 days).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Tips from Google: use **Undo** in the confirmation message (or Ctrl+Z / Command+Z) if you move something by mistake. New folders are not deleted when you undo a move.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

## Research and analysis

You can group files, folders, or emails as sources, then ask Gemini to connect ideas across them—with citations back to your documents.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

Examples Google gives include preparing for a meeting from notes and decks, comparing budget sheets to presentations, and summarizing open action items from meeting notes.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

### Open Ask Gemini with sources (computer)

From a folder:

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Open the folder you want to analyze.
3. In the toolbar, click **Ask Gemini**. The workspace opens with those files listed as sources.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

From scratch:

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the top right, click **Ask Gemini**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

Tip: When you start from scratch, search settings for Drive, Gmail, Chat, and Calendar are on by default. Web search stays off by default.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

### Manage sources and search settings

- **Add sources:** in the left panel under “Your sources” or “Project sources,” click **Add** (including specific Gmail emails).
- **Remove a source:** hover → **Remove**.
- **Temporarily exclude:** uncheck a source without removing it.
- **Suggested files:** click **Suggested** in the left panel. Suggested files are **not** used until you add them.
- **Broader search:** turn on **Let Gemini search for sources**, then optionally leave Drive, Chat, Calendar, Gmail, or Web checked or unchecked.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

### Citations

When Gemini answers from your sources, hover a citation number (for example `[1]`) to see which file it used, or click the file chip to open the document.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

### Conversation history and projects

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **Ask Gemini**.
3. In the left panel, click **History**.
   - From a folder or project: related chats appear under **Suggested**.
   - From elsewhere in Drive: chats appear under **All**, newest first.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

A **project** keeps chats tied to the same set of sources so you can share that foundation with others. Google’s related help covers sharing Gemini research with Drive projects.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

## Create content, images, and audio overviews (computer)

Google notes these features need an eligible Workspace or Google AI plan.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Export Gemini output to Docs or Sheets

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **Ask Gemini**.
3. Ask for a table, proposal, or formatted list.
4. Click **Export to Docs** or **Export to Sheets**.

Files created from Ask Gemini’s immersive workspace save to your **My Drive** root folder.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Audio overviews

**Important:** Audio overviews are currently only available in English. You generate them on the web; you can play them later on web, iOS, and Android. You cannot create them with a side-panel prompt. Limit: **20** audio overviews per day.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Open a PDF (or, for audio generation, also Docs, Slides, Word, PowerPoint, plain text, or videos).
3. At the top right, click **Create audio overview**.
4. When it finishes, click **Play** in the side panel.
5. You also get an email with the file link. Files save in the **Audio overviews** folder in My Drive, named like `[File name] - audio overview - YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC`.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

If you refresh, close the side panel, or close the tab before generation finishes, the file is not created.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Create images

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **Ask Gemini**.
3. Click **Tools** → **Create Images**.
4. Type a prompt.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Generate Docs, Slides, or Sheets

Google documents examples such as meeting agendas, project trackers, and slide decks with a single prompt in **Ask Gemini**. For Slides, keep the tab open until generation finishes (you can switch tabs, but do not close that one). You can reference an existing deck with `@` plus the presentation name.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Skills and Beta features

Some create features (including certain image and generation options) are listed for **Gemini Beta** on work and school accounts (administrator controlled), **Google AI Ultra** and **Google AI Pro** personal accounts, and **Google Workspace Experiments**. Google states desktop and English only for those listed features. If you do not see them, your administrator may not have Beta enabled.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

Skills (for example `@Project Summary`) must be created and turned on in Workspace Studio before you can use them in Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

## Feedback on suggestions

Google Workspace with Gemini is learning and may not support every request. If a suggestion seems inaccurate or unsafe:

1. At the bottom right of the generated text (on a computer), click **Good suggestion** or **Bad suggestion**.
2. For **Bad suggestion**, select the issue and add details if needed.
3. Click **Submit**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

General feedback: **Help** → **Send feedback to Google**. For a legal issue, create a request through Google’s process.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## If you do not see Gemini

- Confirm you are signed into the account that should have access.
- Check whether your school or work administrator has enabled Gemini / Beta features for your organization.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>
- Review Google’s pages on [plans and eligibility](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16685111?hl=en), [disclaimers and data usage](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/16684790?hl=en), and [data protection](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/14615114?hl=en).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-9">9</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

If Drive itself has other problems, see [Fix common Google Drive problems](/guide/fix-google-drive-problems). Still stuck? Submit a Help form so staff can follow up.
