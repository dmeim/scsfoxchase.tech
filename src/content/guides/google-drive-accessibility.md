Google Drive includes display and accessibility options: Dark Mode, screen reader support, toolbar buttons on a computer, and an optional Drive widget on an iPhone Lock Screen.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup><sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

For general Drive layout and offline use, see [Get started with Google Drive](/guide/get-started-google-drive).

## Dark Mode

You can change the theme to make files easier to view. On phones, Dark Mode can also help save battery life.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Computer (including Chromebook)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the upper right, click **Settings**.
3. Under **Appearance**, choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **Device default**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

- **Light** — light background with dark text
- **Dark** — dark background with light text
- **Device default** — match your device’s color scheme<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. At the top left, tap **Menu**.
3. Tap **Settings**.
4. Tap **Choose theme**.
5. Choose **Dark**, **Light**, or **System default**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

Dark Mode in the Drive app follows your device setting. It is available on **iOS 13 and up**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

1. On your iPhone or iPad, turn on **Dark Mode** in system Settings.
2. Open the **Google Drive** app.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Use Drive with a screen reader

You can store, share, and work with files and folders from a computer, tablet, or phone with a screen reader.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Recommended browsers and screen readers (computer)

Google Drive recommends **Chrome** with:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

- **NVDA** or **JAWS** on Windows
- **ChromeVox** on ChromeOS
- **VoiceOver** on Mac

Set the screen reader to treat Drive as a **web application** (not a webpage) so Drive keyboard shortcuts work more easily. Examples:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

- Open the New menu: `Alt` + `N`
- Start a Drive search: `/`
- Move to the file navigation area: `Alt` + `G`, then `N`

#### Screen reader setup tips

- **JAWS** — turn the virtual cursor off (`JAWS` + `Z` until it is off)
- **NVDA** — switch to focus mode (`NVDA` + `Space`)
- **ChromeVox** — turn sticky mode off (press the Search key twice)
- **VoiceOver** — turn QuickNav off (left and right arrows until VoiceOver says “QuickNav Off”)<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Open Drive and move between regions

1. In your browser, go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
2. Move between regions:
   - Windows and Chrome OS: `Ctrl` + `F6` (forward) or `Shift` + `Ctrl` + `F6` (back)
   - Mac: `Command` + `F6` or `Shift` + `Command` + `F6`
3. After each jump, press `Tab` to move into that region.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Main regions include the file and folder table, navigation pane, banner/search controls, and the navigation tree (Home, My Drive, Shared with me, Trash, Storage, and more).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Tip: Home can change based on recent activity. For a more consistent list, set the start page to **My Drive** in Settings (`Ctrl`/`Command` + `Shift` + `S`, then choose **My Drive**).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Keyboard shortcuts list

- Windows and Chrome OS: `Ctrl` + `/`
- Mac: `Command` + `/`<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Open a file

1. Open Drive in the browser.
2. Find the file with first-letter typing or the arrow keys (up/down in list view; left/right in grid view).
3. Press `Enter` to open it.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Switch list and grid view: `Alt` + `V`, then `L` (Windows/Chrome OS) or `Option` + `V`, then `L` (Mac).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Android

You can use Drive with TalkBack, BrailleBack, and Switch Access. With TalkBack:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

1. Tap or swipe to focus a file.
2. Double-tap to open it.
3. For other actions (rename, move): double-tap and hold to select, open **More actions** on the toolbar, then choose an option.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

You can use Drive with **VoiceOver**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Accessibility** → **VoiceOver**.
3. Turn on **VoiceOver**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Google’s full screen reader article covers more tasks (search, create, organize, video playback, and Drive for desktop). Open it when you need deeper steps: [Use Google Drive with a screen reader](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/12169158?hl=en).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Toolbar buttons (computer)

When you select a file or folder on a computer, buttons in the upper left let you take actions. If there is enough screen space, similar buttons can appear when you hover over an item in list layout.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Examples include share, download, rename, add to Starred, move, remove, and more menu actions.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

To act on several items at once:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

1. Click a file or folder.
2. Hold `Ctrl` (Windows) or `Command` (Mac) and click other items.
3. Release the key, then click a toolbar button.

Related: [Share files and folders in Google Drive](/guide/share-files-google-drive), [Delete files in Google Drive](/guide/delete-files-google-drive).

## Drive widget on an iPhone Lock Screen

You can add a Google Drive widget to the Lock Screen so you can open Drive without unlocking first.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Important:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

- Available for **iPhone on iOS 16 or higher**
- The Drive app must already be installed (or the widget will not show in the gallery)

### Add and configure

1. Press and hold the Lock Screen to enter customization.
2. Select **Customize**.
3. Tap the Lock Screen → **Add Widgets**.
4. Scroll to the **Drive** widget and tap it.
5. Drag it to the shortcut bar under the clock.
6. Tap the widget to choose a configuration, then finish with **Done** / set wallpaper as prompted.
7. Tap the Lock Screen until you leave customization mode.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

### Change an existing widget

1. Unlock with Face ID if needed, then press and hold the Lock Screen.
2. Select **Customize**.
3. Tap the existing Drive widget, then tap it again for configurations.
4. Choose a configuration, close the gallery, then leave customization mode.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

## Still stuck?

If Dark Mode, screen reader setup, or the Lock Screen widget does not match what you see, open the matching Google Help article for your device and follow any updated steps. Staff can also follow up through a Help form.
