Beyond basic upload and download, Google Drive can save pages from the web, turn PDFs and photos into editable text, fill some PDF forms, and play or lightly edit video.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

For ordinary file transfers, see [Upload, download, and open files in Google Drive](/guide/upload-download-open-google-drive).

## Save web content (computer, Chrome)

You can save images, videos, audio, PDFs, and other files you find on the web to Drive with the **Save to Google Drive** Chrome extension.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Follow Google’s program policies and copyright laws whenever you save something from the web.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

1. Install the [Save to Google Drive](https://chromewebstore.google.com/) extension from the Chrome Web Store.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
2. Open Google Chrome and open the webpage you want.
3. Save content:
   - **Link, image, HTML5 audio, or video:** Right-click the item → **Save [item] to Google Drive**.
   - **Whole webpage:** In the top right, click the Google Drive extension icon.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

To cancel a download, click **Cancel** in the download window.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

To change how the extension saves, right-click the Save to Google Drive icon → **Options**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Convert a PDF or photo to text (computer)

You can convert image files to text with Google Drive by opening them in Google Docs.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Prepare the file

For best results:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- **Format:** PDFs (including multipage) or photos (`.jpeg`, `.png`, `.gif`)
- **Size:** 2 MB or smaller
- **Text height:** At least 10 pixels
- **Orientation:** Right-side up (rotate before upload if needed)
- **Fonts:** Common fonts such as Arial or Times New Roman
- **Quality:** Sharp image, even lighting, clear contrast

Google Drive detects the document language; many languages are supported.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Convert

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Right-click the file.
3. Click **Open with** → **Google Docs**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

The image is converted to a Docs file. Bold, italics, font size, font type, and line breaks are often kept. Lists, tables, columns, footnotes, and endnotes usually are not.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Fill out a PDF form (Android)

You can fill out some PDF forms in the Google Drive app on Android.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Tap the PDF you want to fill out.
3. At the bottom right, tap **Edit** → **Form Filling**.
   - If you do not see that option, this PDF may not support form filling.
4. Enter your information.
5. At the top right, tap **Save**.
   - To keep the original, choose **More** → **Save as**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Store and play video (computer)

You can store and play videos directly from Google Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Upload a video

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the top left, click **New** → **File upload**.
3. Choose the video file.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

More upload options: [Upload, download, and open files in Google Drive](/guide/upload-download-open-google-drive).

### Find and play

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click the search box → under **Type**, select **Videos** → **All results**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
3. Double-click the file to play.
4. For full screen, click **Fullscreen** at the bottom.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Useful playback extras

- **Open counts:** Select the video → **Details**. Under Analytics you see how many times it was opened in Drive (counts under 5 show as “&lt;5 opens”; over 5,000 as “&gt;5000 opens”). Anyone who can watch the video can see this. Plays inside Docs or Slides are not counted.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- **Copy link:** Open the video → beside **Share**, open the menu → **Copy link**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- **Link to a time:** Scrub to that moment → **Copy link to this time**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- **Thumbnail previews:** On newly uploaded videos, hover the progress bar to preview moments.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- **Comments:** Open the video → leave a **File-level comment**, or scrub to a moment and add a timestamped comment. Indicators on the timeline show existing comments.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Playback in Drive needs third-party cookies turned on in your browser.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Video limits and formats

- Size is limited by your available Drive storage (up to 5 TB if you have purchased that much).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- Minimum size for playback: **4 KB**. Maximum playback resolution: **1920 × 1080**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- Instant playback can work for videos you just uploaded in the same browser session while Drive finishes processing for other devices or people you shared with.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Supported formats (not all work on every device): WebM, MPEG4 / 3GPP / MOV, AVI, MPEGPS, WMV, FLV, MTS, OGG.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### If upload or playback fails

Google’s help lists common checks: stable internet, enough storage, try an Incognito window, another browser, disable conflicting extensions, try the Drive desktop or mobile app, confirm the format, update the browser/app, sign out and back in, or reinstall the Drive app.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Error messages you may see:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

- **Video is unplayable** — file may be corrupted or in an unsupported format; re-upload or convert.
- **Video is still processing** / **currently unavailable** — wait and try again, especially for large files.
- **Playback limits** — too many unsigned-in viewers; sign in to continue.

## Edit a Drive video with Google Vids (computer)

You can use Google Vids for simple edits (trim, music, and similar) on videos stored in Drive that were created outside Vids.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Supported types: **MP4**, **QuickTime**, **OGG**, **WebM**. Individual clips are limited to **35 minutes** and **4 GB**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Select the video to open it in preview.
3. At the top right, click **Open**.
   - A new Vids project opens with your video as the first scene.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

To save the edited video as MP4: **File** → **Download as MP4**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

## Stack scanner app (legacy)

Google removed support for **Stack: PDF Scanner** starting the week of September 23, 2024. Scanning and organizing documents is meant to happen in the Google Drive app instead.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Bulk “Export all Stack Documents to Google Drive” is no longer available. You can still export one document at a time:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

1. Open the Stack app.
2. Open the document.
3. Tap **Share**.
4. Choose Google Drive (or another app).
