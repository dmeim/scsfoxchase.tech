These basics cover **exporting a copy of your Drive-related data** and **blocking people** who share files with you. For full policy text, use Google’s own Help pages linked at the end.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Export your data

You can export and download data from Google Drive, including items from Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drawings, Sites, Drive, Forms, and Vids. Use the archive for your records or to move the data elsewhere. You can download files that have not been deleted.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

If you use a **work or school account**, some data might not be available for download.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Important:** Trying to export files that exceed Google’s maximum size limits may cause the export to fail.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Google’s Help article lists what can be included (for example: document text and comments, spreadsheet cells and formulas, slides and embedded media, Drive files and folders, form questions and responses, and Vids video content). Open the article for the full checklist and the current download steps.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Related: [Upload, download, and open files in Google Drive](/guide/upload-download-open-google-drive).

## Block someone in Google Drive

If you do not want file shares from someone in Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides, you can block them.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

When you block someone:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

- They cannot share files with you.
- You cannot share files with them until you unblock them.
- They cannot access your files, and you cannot access theirs.
- You stop getting their comment notifications in Docs, Sheets, or Slides — unless you subscribe to notifications for **all** comments, in which case you still get those.

There may be a short delay before a block or unblock takes effect in Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

If you use Google Drive for **work or school**, you cannot block someone in your domain, or in a separate but trusted domain.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Block the file sharer (from Gmail)

1. Go to [mail.google.com](https://mail.google.com).
2. Open a Drive sharing email.
3. At the bottom of the email, click **Block the sender**.
4. In the new tab, select the account where you want to create the block.
5. Click **Block**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Block the file owner (from Drive)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Right-click a file.
3. Click **Report or block** → **Block**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Unblock a person

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the top right, click your profile picture → **Manage your Google Account**.
3. Click **People & sharing** → **Blocked**.
4. Next to that person’s name, select **Remove**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>
