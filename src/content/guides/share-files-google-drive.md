You can share files and folders stored in Google Drive and choose whether people can **view**, **comment**, or **edit**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

For shared drives, limited access folders, and related access topics, see [Shared drives and access limits in Google Drive](/guide/shared-drives-and-access-google-drive).

## Permission roles (My Drive)

These roles apply to files in **My Drive**:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

| Role | View | Comment | Edit | Download | Share | Change permissions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No | Yes by default (owner can control) | No | No |
| Commenter | Yes | Yes | No | Yes by default (owner can control) | No | No |
| Editor | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (cannot change the owner) | Yes by default (owner can control) |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Limits Google documents: a Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Vids file can be edited on up to **100** open tabs or devices at once; you can share a single file with up to **600** email addresses.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

School tip: with a work or school account, you might not be able to share outside your organization.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

## Share a file

### Computer (including Chromebook)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Select the file you want to share.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Enter the email address (or Google Group) you want to share with.
5. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
6. Optionally add a message, or uncheck notify if you do not want an email.
7. Click **Send** or **Share**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Share more than one file at once: hold **Shift**, select two or more files, then click **Share** at the top left.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Share a link (anyone with the link)

1. Select the file.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Under **General access**, open the menu.
4. Choose **Anyone with the link**.
5. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
6. Click **Copy link**, then **Done**.
7. Paste the link where you want to share it.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

When you share a link, your name and email are visible as the owner. People who are not signed in may appear as anonymous animals in the file.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app (or Docs, Sheets, or Slides).
2. Next to the file name, tap **More**.
3. Tap **Share**.
4. Enter a recipient.
5. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
6. Tap **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

To share several files on Android, put them in a folder and share the folder, or use drive.google.com on a computer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

**Anyone with the link** on Android:

1. Select the file → **Share**.
2. Under **General access**, tap **Change**.
3. Select **Anyone with the link**.
4. Choose a role, tap **Copy link**, then **Back**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Next to the file you want to share, tap **More** → **Share**.
3. Enter the email address or Google Group.
4. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
5. Tap **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

To share several files on iPhone or iPad, add them to a folder and share the folder, or use a computer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

**Anyone with the link** uses the same **Manage access** / **General access** → **Change** → **Anyone with the link** path as Android.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Share a folder

When you share a folder, files and subfolders inside get the same sharing settings. New files added later inherit those permissions too.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

What people can do in a shared folder:

- **Editor**: open, edit, delete, or move files; add files to the folder.
- **Commenter**: comment and suggest; cannot change or reshare folder items.
- **Viewer**: see the folder and open files inside.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

You cannot give someone **less** access to one file than they have on the parent folder. Trying to reduce a single file usually prompts you to change the whole folder instead. For stricter access on part of a folder, use a limited access folder — see [Shared drives and access limits in Google Drive](/guide/shared-drives-and-access-google-drive).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Computer

1. Go to Google Drive.
2. Select the folder.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Enter the email address or Google Group.
5. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
6. Choose whether to notify people.
7. Click **Send** or **Share**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

You can also share with a Google Group: members gain access when they join the group and lose it when they leave.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Select the folder → **Share**.
3. Enter the email address.
4. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
5. Choose whether to notify people.
6. Tap **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

For link-style access: next to the folder, tap **More** → **Manage access** → **General access** → **Change**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Select the folder → **Share**.
3. Enter the email address or Google Group.
4. Choose a role and whether to notify people.
5. Tap **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

**Manage access** for the folder works the same way as Android (**More** → **Manage access**).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

Tip: a shared folder may not show under **Shared with me** until you open it from the invitation or link.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Stop, limit, or change sharing

The owner and anyone with edit access can change sharing. If you remove or reduce someone’s access and they get access through a parent folder, Drive may change permissions for the whole folder.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Computer — remove someone

1. Find the file or folder in Google Drive.
2. Right-click it (or open sharing) and use **Manage access** if you can make changes.
3. Click **Share**.
4. Next to the person’s name, open the menu and choose **Remove access**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Computer — restrict link access

Setting **General access** to **Restricted** means only people with access can open the file. That can override inherited parent folder permissions.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

1. Select the file or folder → **Share**.
2. Under **General access**, choose **Restricted**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Computer — limit download, print, copy, or resharing

If you own the file:

1. Open **Share**.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Under people who can download, copy, and print, adjust the role checkboxes.
4. To stop editors from changing permissions or resharing, uncheck **Editors can change permissions and share**, then **Done**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

Folder permissions still apply to files inside. Preventing resharing on a file does not stop someone from sharing the parent folder that contains it.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

### Android

**Remove someone:**

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Select a file or folder → **Manage access**.
3. Find the person → tap them → **Remove**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

## Transfer ownership

You own files you create or upload. You can transfer ownership only to someone you already shared with.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

Work or school accounts: you can usually transfer only to someone in your organization, and the new owner may not need to accept.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

### Transfer a Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Vids file (computer)

1. Open Google Drive and open the file.
2. At the upper right, click **Share**.
3. Next to the person’s name, open the menu → **Transfer ownership**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

Until they accept (when acceptance is required), you stay the owner. After they accept, you become an **Editor** and they can remove you. You can cancel before they accept with **Cancel ownership transfer**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

### Transfer a folder (computer)

1. Go to drive.google.com.
2. Select the folder (Shift-click for more than one).
3. Click **Share**.
4. Next to the person’s name → **Transfer ownership** → **Yes**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

When you transfer a folder, you still own the files inside unless you transfer those separately.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

On Android or iPhone, Google’s help says to transfer folder ownership from drive.google.com on a computer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

After a personal-account transfer, the file leaves your My Drive storage and counts toward the new owner’s storage. You cannot transfer from a personal account to a work or school account.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-10">10</a></sup>

## Anonymous or unknown people in a file

If a file is shared publicly or with **Anyone with the link**, you may see names you do not recognize or **anonymous animals**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

That can happen when:

- The file is shared with a mailing list.
- Someone without a Google Account (or not signed in) opens it.
- An editor or link holder shared it further.
- Someone changed their Google Account name (check **Share** for their email).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

People you invite individually show by name. People who only have the link often show as anonymous animals.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

To tighten access: set **General access** to **Restricted**, and prevent others from resharing files you own (steps above).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

On work or school accounts, people in your domain usually show by name; people outside who are not signed in can still appear as anonymous animals if external sharing is allowed.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-11">11</a></sup>

## Share with visitors (work or school)

If your organization turns on visitor sharing, you can invite people who do not have Google Accounts. Visitors verify with a PIN and can collaborate for **7 days**, then re-verify from the original email if they need longer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-12">12</a></sup>

Visitors are not the same as Workspace **Guests** (domain-hosted guest accounts).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-12">12</a></sup>

1. Sign in with your work or school account.
2. Open Drive, Docs, Sheets, or Slides.
3. Select the file → **Share**.
4. Enter their email under **Share with people and groups**.
5. Choose **Viewer**, **Commenter**, or **Editor**.
6. Click **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-12">12</a></sup>
