Deleted something from Google Drive by mistake? If it is still in **Trash**, you can restore it yourself.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Pick the section that matches how you open Drive.

## Computer (including Chromebook)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. On the left, click **Trash**.
   - Deleted files are listed there.
   - To see when a file was deleted, sort by **Trashed date**.
3. Right-click the file.
4. Click **Restore**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Android

If you deleted something recently in the Google Drive app (or Drive for desktop), you may be able to restore it from Trash.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Tap the menu (three lines) at the top left.
3. Tap **Trash**.
4. Next to the file you want to recover, tap **Menu**
   <img class="guide-inline-icon" src="/guides/recover-deleted-file-google-drive/menu-more.png" alt="" width="18" height="18" />.
5. Tap **Restore**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

3. Tap **Trash**.
4. Next to the file you want to recover, tap **Menu**
   <img class="guide-inline-icon" src="/guides/recover-deleted-file-google-drive/menu-more.png" alt="" width="18" height="18" />.

## Timing and limits

- On a computer, files stay in Trash for **30 days**, then they are deleted permanently.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- Files that were permanently deleted from Gmail, Google Photos, or WhatsApp backups cannot be recovered through this Drive process.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## If Trash does not have the file

Google’s help article shows additional recovery options when you are signed into a Google Account. Sign in, open the article for your device, and follow any extra steps that appear.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

If you still cannot get the file back, submit a Help form so staff can follow up.
