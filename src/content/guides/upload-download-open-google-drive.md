You can upload, download, open, and store many kinds of files in Google Drive. Uploaded files use storage space in your Drive, even when you put them in a folder owned by someone else.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

For PDFs, video, saving web pages, and related extras, see [PDFs, video, and web content in Google Drive](/guide/pdfs-video-and-web-in-google-drive).

## What you can store

Drive can store any file type. Common types you can preview include documents, images, audio, video, PDFs, Microsoft Office files, and archives.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

Size limits Google publishes include:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

- **Google Docs** — up to about 1.02 million characters (or up to 50 MB when converting a text document to Docs format)
- **Google Sheets** — up to 10 million cells (or 18,278 columns)
- **Google Slides** — up to 100 MB for presentations converted to Slides
- **Other files** — up to 5 TB

The Drive preview is a scaled-down view and may look slightly different when you open the full file.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-7">7</a></sup>

## Upload files

### Computer (including Chromebook)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. At the top left, click **New**, then **File upload** or **Folder upload**.
3. Choose the file or folder.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Or drag files into an open Drive folder in the browser.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Drive for desktop:** After you install the app, drag files into the Google Drive folder on your computer. They upload and appear on drive.google.com.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Same name:** If you upload a file with the same name as one already in Drive, Drive may upload it as a revision. To keep both, choose **Keep both files**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

**Convert Office uploads to Google formats:** On a computer, open [Drive settings](https://drive.google.com/drive/settings) and check **Convert uploads to Google Docs editor format**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Tip: When you upload with the web app or Drive for desktop, creation time is the upload time. Modification time is when the file was last changed on the source device, so it can be earlier than creation time.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Tap **New**, then **Upload**.
3. Find and tap the files to upload.
4. Uploaded files appear in **My Drive** until you move them.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

To transfer only over Wi-Fi: open the app menu → **Settings** → under **Data usage**, turn **Transfer files only over Wi-Fi** on or off (shown when the device can use mobile data).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

You can change the “convert uploads to Google Docs format” setting only from a computer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Tap **Add**, then **File upload**.
3. Find and tap the files to upload.
4. Tap **Location** to choose the folder.
5. Optionally tap **File Name** to rename.
6. Tap **Upload**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Download files

If a file looks suspicious, Drive may warn you — be careful before you download.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Computer (including Chromebook)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click the file (hold Command on Mac or Ctrl on Windows to select more).
3. Right-click, then click **Download**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

You cannot drag a Drive file or folder straight to your desktop.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

To export a Google Doc, Sheet, Slides, or Vids file in another format (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, JPEG, and more): open the file → **File** → **Download** → choose a format.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

If download is blocked, the owner may have turned off print, download, or copy for viewers and commenters.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

**Chrome cookie tip:** Drive web downloads need third-party cookies for Drive. In Chrome, allow third-party cookies for `drive.google.com` under cookie settings if downloads fail.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Android

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Next to the file name, tap **More**.
3. Tap **Download**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

### iPhone and iPad

1. Open the **Google Drive** app.
2. Next to the file, tap **More**.
3. Tap **Download** (you may need to scroll), or use **Open in** / **Save to Files** for another app.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

To save a photo or video to the camera roll: **More** → **Send a copy** → **Save image** or **Save video**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-6">6</a></sup>

## View and open files

On drive.google.com, double-click a file:<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

- Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, or Drawings open in that app.
- Videos, Microsoft Office files, audio, and photos open in Drive preview.
- Shared Gemini chats, canvases, or generated media open in the Gemini web app (Workspace accounts on Drive on the web).

You can set whether PDFs open in a new tab or in preview in the same tab in Drive settings.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

To open with another app on a computer: right-click the file → **Open with** → choose an app. Opening with installed desktop apps needs a recent Drive for desktop and Application Launcher by Google.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>

If a file looks suspicious, Drive may warn you before you open it.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-8">8</a></sup>
