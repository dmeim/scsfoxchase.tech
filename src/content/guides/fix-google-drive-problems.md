Having trouble opening a file, editing a Doc, or finding something that vanished? Start with the checks below, then jump to spam, bulk restore, or support if you still need help.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## First checks when Drive will not load

### Temporary errors and outages

If you see **Temporary Error (502)** when opening files, Google says documents are temporarily unavailable — wait a bit and try again.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Also check the [Google Workspace Status Dashboard](https://www.google.com/appsstatus/dashboard/). A known outage for Drive shows as a red dot next to the product; click it for details.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Connection and browser

If you see **Trying to connect**, or Drive feels very slow on the web:

1. Check your internet connection. Try another network if you can.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
2. Confirm you are on a supported browser and OS. Drive works with the **2 most recent versions** of major browsers. Turn on cookies and JavaScript.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
3. Google recommends Chrome for Drive, but you can also try Firefox, Internet Explorer, or Safari.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

### Offline access

If the connection is weak, set up offline access so you can view and edit documents without the internet. Changes sync when you are back online.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

If offline is already on and files still will not open, turn it off and on again: open **Settings**, find **Offline**, and uncheck or check **Sync to edit offline**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

More offline setup: [Get started with Google Drive](/guide/get-started-google-drive).

### Extra computer checks

- Virus scanners and ad blockers on your device can interfere with Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Make sure they are not blocking Google Workspace.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>
- On Firefox, Enhanced Tracking Protection can make files display incorrectly. Follow Firefox’s steps under “What to do if a site seems broken,” then you can report the site again.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

Still stuck after these steps? Send feedback from Drive, or see sync help in [Use Google Drive for desktop](/guide/use-google-drive-for-desktop).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-1">1</a></sup>

## Errors while editing Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Vids

Messages like **An error occurred**, **Encountered an error…**, or **A server error occurred…** can block edits. If you see those, wait about **5 minutes**, then reload the page.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Troubleshooting Google lists:

- Turn off browser extensions.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>
- Clear browsing data.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>
- Improve your internet connection (move closer to Wi‑Fi, or use a cable when you can).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>
- Update outdated software or your browser. Try the same file on another device — if it works there, the issue may be this computer.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>
- Try a different browser.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Important: Vids does not currently support editing or commenting in Safari. Playback works once the video is processed.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Tip: To reduce copy-and-paste image errors on a phone, update your apps.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

### Keep working while you fix the file

Share edit access with another account and open the file there (you can share from Drive without opening the file).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Or make a copy:

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Right-click the file.
3. Click **Make a copy**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

Important: A copy loses some features, such as version history.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

You can also **Download** the file, then **New** → **File upload** to put a new copy back in Drive — that also loses version history.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

#### Sheets-only options

- If a copy still fails, Google documents using `IMPORTRANGE()` to pull data into a new spreadsheet (you need the original URL and tab name). Formulas from the original Sheet are not imported that way.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>
- If a Sheet is too large to edit and becomes read-only, copy individual tabs to a new spreadsheet (**Copy to** → **New spreadsheet**), or download / paste into a new document.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

If reload errors continue, use the Docs Editors Help Community, or open the file and choose **Help** → **Help us improve**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-5">5</a></sup>

## Mark or unmark spam

Drive can move files or folders it strongly suspects are unwanted into **Spam**. You can also move items in and out yourself. After more than **30 days** in Spam, items are permanently removed from your Drive.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

Files or folders you report as spam:

- Stop generating comment notifications.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
- Are not discoverable in Drive Search or suggestions.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
- Are reviewed by Google for policy violations.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

Important: You cannot report files or folders that you or members of your organization own.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

### Mark as spam (computer)

1. Open Drive.
2. Right-click a file or folder in **Shared with me** (or select several with the checkboxes, then right-click).
3. Click **Block or report** → **Report**. You can also drag items into **Spam**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>
4. Choose the type of unwanted content. You can also choose whether to block the user.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

Important: If you mark a folder as spam, everything inside it is marked as spam too.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

### Unmark spam

1. Open **Spam**.
2. Select the file or folder, then click **Not spam** (or right-click → **Not spam**).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

To unmark items inside a spam folder: open Spam, expand the folder, select the files, and unmark them.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

### Remove spam forever

In Spam, choose **Remove all spam files or folders now**, or select specific items and **Remove forever**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-3">3</a></sup>

## Restore many file versions at once (Beta)

The **Restore file versions** tool can roll multiple files back to a previous version. Google Drive keeps the last **25 days** of revisions. Use this after ransomware encryption, to undo recent changes, or to compare versions.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

For deleted files, use [Recover a deleted file in Google Drive](/guide/recover-deleted-file-google-drive) instead.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Important:

- For every selected file, previous versions are **copied** and become the new current versions. No versions are deleted.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>
- Files created in Google Apps like Docs and Slides are not affected by ransomware in the way described there and will not appear in this tool.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

### Use Restore file versions

1. In your browser, go to Google Drive.
2. At the top right, open **Settings** → **Restore file versions**.
3. Review the change history and select the version to roll back to. You cannot pick individual files in that step.
4. Click **Restore** and wait until that restore finishes before starting another.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

If you roll back too far, recover recent edits from that file’s own version history.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

Drive for desktop may pause syncing if it detects ransomware encryption. After you restore, you can re-enable syncing — see [Use Google Drive for desktop](/guide/use-google-drive-for-desktop).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-4">4</a></sup>

## Contact Google Drive support

Google’s contact article points you to common fixes first:

- Cannot find or recover a file → search Drive, restore from Trash within **30 days**, check the device Recycle Bin / Trash for locally deleted uploads, or use Photos help for Photos (Drive support cannot recover Photos).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>
- Sharing → Google’s contact article points to share help for files and folders.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>
- Drive for desktop → [Use Google Drive for desktop](/guide/use-google-drive-for-desktop).<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

If you still need help, use the help forum, feedback, or support service.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

### Send feedback from Drive (computer)

1. Go to [drive.google.com](https://drive.google.com).
2. Click **Help**.
3. Click **Send feedback**.
4. Enter your feedback, then click **Send**.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

Some recovery options on Google’s page appear only when you are signed into a Google Account.<sup class="guide-fn"><a href="#source-2">2</a></sup>

If you still cannot get unstuck after these steps, submit a Help form so school staff can follow up.
